# Project-Based AI Learning

The shared language for a project-based curriculum covering self-directed learning,
Python, language-model training, and practical AI agent use.

## Language

**Curriculum**:
The complete sequence of learning objectives, activities, projects, and mastery
checks. A collection of links or lesson pages alone is not a curriculum.
_Avoid_: Resource list as a synonym for curriculum.

**Study Day**:
A planned unit of eight hours of active learner effort, excluding breaks and
unattended agent or training runtime. It may include review and assessment rather
than new material.
_Avoid_: Eight hours of screen time.

**Lesson**:
A coherent learning unit with prerequisites, an objective, explanations, practice,
feedback, and a mastery criterion. A lesson is not necessarily one study day.
_Avoid_: Page as a synonym for lesson.

**Learning Project**:
A learner-built artifact addressing an explicit problem, accompanied by tests,
evaluation evidence, and an explanation of limitations.
_Avoid_: Running a demo as a synonym for completing a project.

**Agent Tool Track**:
One of the alternative tool-specific routes through the shared agent-use learning
objectives. The learner completes one track, not all available tracks.
_Avoid_: Mandatory tool stack.

**Vibe Coding Practice**:
Agent-assisted project work in which the learner specifies intent, manages
changes and permissions, inspects outputs, and verifies behavior.
_Avoid_: Unchecked code generation.

**Independent Mastery Check**:
A task performed without AI assistance that provides evidence of the learner's
understanding or implementation ability.
_Avoid_: AI-assisted completion as proof of independent mastery.

**Agent-Assisted Engineering Check**:
A task that evaluates the learner's ability to direct, inspect, correct, and
validate an agent's work. It is distinct from an independent mastery check.
_Avoid_: Agent output quality as proof of the learner's unaided knowledge.

**Learning Evidence**:
Artifacts and observed results supporting a specific learning claim, with enough
context to understand what was and was not checked.
_Avoid_: Completion checkbox as proof of competence.

**Source Resource**:
A third-party course, transcript, paper, repository, or document selected to
support learning, with its provenance and usage conditions retained.
_Avoid_: Publicly accessible as a synonym for freely redistributable.

**Language Edition**:
A localized presentation of the same learning unit, with equivalent objectives,
exercises, answers, and acceptance criteria.
_Avoid_: Translated interface as a synonym for a complete language edition.

**Course Readiness Gate**:
The acceptance boundary before the learner begins the scheduled course:
the complete curriculum and all required language editions and platform
capabilities must be delivered and checked, with limitations disclosed.
_Avoid_: Pages generated as a synonym for course readiness.
